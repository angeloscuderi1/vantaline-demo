Understanding your bill

Answers to common billing questions.

Why can my bill change?

Prices vary based on your services, equipment, taxes, and any promotions or discounts on your account.

How do I read my statement?

Your statement shows monthly service charges, one-time charges, and taxes and fees.

When is my payment due?

Your due date is shown on the first page of your statement.

Can I set up Autopay?

Yes. Go to Billing and choose Autopay.
